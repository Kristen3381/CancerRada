Breast Cancer Prevention Tips

Regular Self-Exams

Perform monthly breast self-exams to familiarize yourself with how your breasts normally look and feel. Report any changes to your healthcare provider immediately.

Healthy Diet

Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains. Limit alcohol consumption and processed foods. Maintain a healthy weight to reduce breast cancer risk.

Physical Activity

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week to maintain a healthy weight and reduce risk. Even brisk walking can make a difference.

Regular Check-ups

Schedule regular clinical breast exams and mammograms as recommended by your healthcare provider based on your age and risk factors.
