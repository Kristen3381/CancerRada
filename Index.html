Financial Assistance for Breast Cancer Care

Several organizations in Kenya provide financial support for screening, diagnosis, and treatment of breast cancer.

National Hospital Insurance Fund (NHIF)

Covers breast cancer screening, chemotherapy, and surgery for members.

Contact: 020 275 6000

Faraja Cancer Support Trust

Provides financial assistance for treatment and supportive care.

Contact: 0709 932 000

Women 4 Cancer Early Detection

Offers subsidized screening and diagnostic services.

Contact: 0722 205 255

Additional Resources
